# What Enterprise AI would do in their app

As you learn the job (Consult) and draw the app (Prototype), notice which parts the Enterprise AI
platform would do when it's built for real. It's what makes the app more than a prototype, and
it tells the build team where to start.

| When the app needs to… | Enterprise AI has | In their words |
|---|---|---|
| Read documents, drawings, forms or emails and pull out what matters | **Content Understanding** (classify, extract, summarise) | "Enterprise AI reads the drawings and lists the quantities" |
| Answer questions from their own documents | **AI Search** over their content, with sources | "The assistant answers from your policies, and shows where" |
| Check something against rules or a checklist | **Document checking and AI checklists** | "Each document is checked against your rules as it arrives" |
| Move work between people, with sign-off and an audit trail | **Workflows and approvals** | "It goes to an officer to sign off, and every decision is recorded" |
| Track targets and how the work is going | **Goals and targets** | "You'll see how many quotes went out the same morning" |
| Keep records, files and history safely | **Workspace data and file storage**, behind their sign-in | "Everything stays in your Enterprise AI workspace" |
| Control who gets in and what each person sees | **Workspace sign-in and roles** | "Only your estimators can see prices" |

- **Keep it in their language.** Mention it once, where it helps: in the message that opens the
  draft ("When we build it, Enterprise AI's document reading does the quantities"), not as a
  list of products.
- **Write it down** under `## On Enterprise AI` in BRIEF.md: each part of the app and what the
  platform would do. Build starts from it.
- **Don't invent capabilities.** If nothing here fits a part of the app, say it would be built
  specially.
